Help & FAQ

Inspect a texture in three steps

  1. Import. Drag an image into the inspector, paste one with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on macOS), or choose a file.
  2. Inspect. Use Tile, Mirror and Seam Map to compare the result with the original shown in the sidebar.
  3. Export. Download a PNG preview.

Inspection modes

TilePreview repetition using a 2×2 to 6×6 grid.
MirrorCompare alternately mirrored repetition with normal tiling.
Seam mapHighlight RGB differences along opposite borders.

Source preview

The unchanged original stays visible in the left sidebar while you switch between inspection modes.

Common questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. Images are decoded and processed inside your browser. Tilecheck does not upload your textures.

Does Seam Map guarantee seamlessness?

No. Seam Map highlights differences along opposite edges, but visual inspection is still essential, especially with alpha, normal maps and repeating motifs.

Can Tilecheck repair seams?

No. Tilecheck helps you inspect visible seams, but does not change or repair your source texture.

Which images are supported?

PNG, JPEG, WebP and AVIF, depending on your browser. Maximum file size: 25 MB; maximum image size: 24 megapixels.

Can it run offline?

Yes. Download the repository and open index.html. No installation or build tooling is required.

Need help or found a bug?

Report issues through GitHub. Include your browser, texture format and reproduction steps. Please avoid sharing private textures in public issues.
